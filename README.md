# Multi-Jurisdictional HR Compliance: Coforge–Encora Merger

## Project Overview

This project analyses the multi-jurisdictional HR compliance challenges explored in our Strategic Human Resource Management (SHRM) case study on the Coforge–Encora merger.

As organisations operate across multiple countries, HR teams must consider differences in employment laws, compensation requirements, employee benefits, working hours, taxation, and employee rights.

The analysis focuses on how HR can support global integration while addressing country-specific legal and regulatory requirements.

## Research Question

**How should HR navigate multi-jurisdictional labour laws and compliance requirements following an acquisition?**

## Key Areas of Analysis

* Differences in employment laws across India, the United States, and Latin America.
* Country-specific requirements relating to compensation, benefits, working hours, taxation, and employee rights.
* Challenges in implementing consistent global HR policies.
* Coordination between local HR teams and legal or compliance specialists.
* HR compliance reviews, policy updates, and employee training.

## Proposed HR Compliance Framework

The case study explores a structured response to potential compliance issues:

1. Identify the country and nature of the compliance concern.
2. Involve the relevant local HR and legal or compliance teams.
3. Stop or correct the non-compliant practice, as appropriate.
4. Conduct a compliance review to determine whether similar issues exist elsewhere.
5. Update relevant policies and processes.
6. Train managers and HR teams to reduce the risk of recurrence.

## Strategic HR Implications

The analysis highlights the importance of balancing global HR consistency with local legal requirements.

A coordinated approach can help organisations identify compliance risks, improve accountability, and support effective workforce integration across geographical boundaries.

## Conclusion

Multi-jurisdictional HR compliance is an important consideration in cross-border acquisitions. HR teams must understand applicable local requirements, collaborate with legal and compliance specialists, and establish appropriate processes to support lawful and effective global workforce integration.

This case study strengthened my understanding of the relationship between global HR strategy, regulatory compliance, and organisational integration.
